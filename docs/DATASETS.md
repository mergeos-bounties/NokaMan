# Datasets

Public ESL/learner corpora relevant to NokaMan assessment and training.

| # | Corpus | Description | License | Link |
|---|--------|-------------|---------|------|
| 1 | **ELLIPSE** | ~6,500 ELL writing samples scored for holistic and analytic proficiency (cohesion, syntax, vocabulary, phraseology, grammar, conventions) | CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 | https://github.com/scrosseye/ELLIPSE-Corpus |
| 2 | **PELIC** | University of Pittsburgh English Language Institute Corpus — 4.2M-word longitudinal learner corpus (written & spoken), EAP context | CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 | https://github.com/ELI-Data-Mining-Group/PELIC-dataset |
| 3 | **CEFR-ASAG** | Short answers written by learners of English, graded with CEFR levels; 299 answers labeled by CEFR-certified examiners | CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 | https://github.com/anaistack/cefr-asag-corpus |
| 4 | **COREFL** | CORpus of English as a Foreign Language — 530K+ words from L1 Spanish and L1 German learners of English, with native control subcorpora | CC BY-NC-ND 3.0 ES | https://corefl.learnercorpora.com/ |
| 5 | **EFCAMDAT** | EF-Cambridge Open Language Database — 1.18M+ texts from 174K+ learners worldwide, 16 CEFR-aligned proficiency levels | Academic access required (free for researchers) | https://ef-lab.mmll.cam.ac.uk/EFCAMDAT.html |
| 6 | **LOCNESS** | Louvain Corpus of Native English Essays — 324K words of native British/American essays (reference/control corpus) | Non-commercial; cite CECL | https://www.learnercorpusassociation.org/resources/tools/locness-corpus |
| 7 | **ICNALE** | International Corpus Network of Asian Learners of English — 10K topic-controlled essays and speeches from 10 Asian regions + L1 English controls | Open access (registration required) | https://language.sakura.ne.jp/icnale/ |
| 8 | **ICLE** | International Corpus of Learner English — ~4M+ words from advanced learners across 25+ L1 backgrounds | Free for non-profit educational purposes | https://corpora.uclouvain.be/cecl/icle |
| 9 | **NUS Corpus of Learner English** | Large annotated corpus of learner English with error annotations, from NUS | Research license (free for research) | https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/~nlp/corpora.html |
| 10 | **CEDEL2** | Corpus Escrito del Español L2 — 1.5M+ words from learners of Spanish (15 L1 backgrounds); useful as a model for multilingual learner corpora | CC BY-NC-ND 3.0 ES | https://cedel2.learnercorpora.com/ |

## Notes

- **Do not redistribute** restricted media (audio, video) without explicit permission.
- Corpora marked "Academic access required" or "registration required" must be requested from the respective providers.
- Always check the latest license terms on the official website before use.
- CEDEL2 and COREFL follow the Open Data Science philosophy and are freely downloadable for research/teaching.
